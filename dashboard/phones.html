Electron

Text a photo.Get a technician.

Electron is an AI dispatcher for small trade shops, run entirely over iMessage. Send a photo of the problem. Electron reads it, picks the right technician, and books the visit. No app, no account, no phone tag.

Text a photo to+1 (628) 997-9661
  1. Text a photo and one sentence about what's wrong.
  2. Electron reads it, texts back a safety tip, and scores every technician.
  3. The best match gets the job in iMessage and replies YES or NO.
  4. You OK the estimate and get a time booked on their calendar.
